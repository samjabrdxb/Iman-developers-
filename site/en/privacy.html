Privacy policy

Version of 2026-11-02

This policy explains how Sam Jabr, Senior Sales Manager at IMAN Developers (Dubai, UAE), "we", handles the personal data of visitors to https://samjabrdxb.github.io/Iman-developers-/en/.

1. What we collect

  • The name, phone number, budget, preferred layout and contact method you enter in the enquiry form.
  • Technical visit data (cookies, IP address, browser and device details, referring source) collected by Google Ads and Yandex Metrica.

2. Why we use it

  • To contact you and answer your enquiry: matching a property and sending prices, floor plans and payment terms.
  • To measure visits and improve the website and our advertising.

3. Legal basis

We process your data with the consent you give by sending the form. You can withdraw it at any time by writing to us.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your data or share it with third parties, except the services the website needs to work: the service that delivers form enquiries by e-mail, WhatsApp and Telegram when you contact us through them, Google Ads and Yandex Metrica. If you buy, your data may be shared with IMAN Developers and the UAE authorities (Dubai Land Department) as far as the purchase requires.

5. How long we keep it

Until the purpose is fulfilled or you withdraw consent, and no longer than 3 years after your last contact.

6. Your rights

You can ask what data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, and withdraw your consent.

7. Cookies

The website uses Google and Yandex Metrica cookies. You can turn cookies off in your browser settings; the website will keep working.
